Chapter 1

The Psychopathology
of Everyday Things

Signifiers are the most important addition to the chapter, a concept first introduced in my book Living with Complexity. The first edition had a focus upon affordances, but although affordances Preface to the Revised Edition make sense for interaction with physical objects, they are confusing when dealing with virtual ones.

As a result, affordances have created much confusion in the world of design. Affordances define what actions are possible. Signifiers specify how people discover those possibilities: signifiers are signs, perceptible signals of what can be done.

Signifiers are of far more importance to designers than are affordances. Hence, the extended treatment. I added a very brief section on HCD, a term that didn't yet exist when the first edition was published, although looking back we see that the entire book was about HCD.

Other than that, the chapter is the same, and although all the photographs and drawings are new, the ideas remain simple: make things visible, make feedback clear, and design for the way people actually think.
